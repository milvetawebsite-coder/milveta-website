Our Story

Milveta began with a simple observation: the grains our grandparents grew up on — ragi, jowar, bajra, and a dozen other native millets — had quietly disappeared from most modern kitchens, replaced by convenience and refined flour.

We set out to bring them back, not as a nostalgic throwback, but as a precisely formulated everyday staple — blending 22 traditional grains, pulses, and seeds into a single spoonful that fits a modern routine as easily as it honors an old one.

WHY WE EXIST

Our Purpose

Good nutrition shouldn't require a nutrition degree to access. Our purpose is to translate the dense science of millet-based nutrition into one clear, everyday product — so that families across every kind of household can add real, natural nourishment to their routine without added complexity or added sugar.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Global Context

Millets aren't just an Indian heritage grain — they're a global response to two connected problems: rising lifestyle-driven nutrition gaps, and a food system under pressure from a changing climate. Millets need a fraction of the water of rice or wheat, grow in poor soil, and still deliver dense nutrition — which is why the United Nations named 2023 the International Year of Millets.

Milveta sits inside that global shift: sourcing domestically, formulating locally, and putting that same resilient grain family into a format any household, anywhere, can use daily.

SCIENCE MEETS TRADITION

The Nutritional Power of Millets

Gram for gram, millets carry more fiber, more micronutrients, and a gentler impact on blood sugar than most refined staples — the reason nutritionists keep returning to them for weight management, digestion, and sustained energy. Milveta concentrates 22 of these grains, pulses, and seeds into a single daily spoonful, so the science doesn't stay in a research paper — it shows up on your breakfast table.
