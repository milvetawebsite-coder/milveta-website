OUR STORY

Can we bring the wisdom of traditional Indian grains back into modern everyday life?

For generations, our ancestors built their lives around simple, traditional foods — millets, whole grains, pulses, seeds and other naturally nourishing ingredients.

Their food was not simply about taste or filling the stomach. Food was an important part of everyday life, providing nourishment and energy alongside an active lifestyle.

As lifestyles have changed, food habits have changed too. Modern life demands convenience, speed and taste. In this transformation, the nutritional purpose of everyday food can sometimes be overlooked.

Milveta was born from a simple thought: Can we bring the wisdom of traditional Indian grains back into modern everyday life?

Our answer is Milveta.

The Global Millet Movement

From Ancient Grains to the Future of Food

Millets are not merely grains from the past. They are increasingly becoming part of a global conversation about the future of food. Millets gained significant global attention in 2023 through the International Year of Millets and related global conferences and initiatives, with discussions spanning nutrition, food security, climate resilience, sustainable agriculture, markets and farmer livelihoods.
